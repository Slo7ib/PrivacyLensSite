Why use our extension?

Privacy policies are long, vague, and intentionally hard to understand. Privacy Lens turns them into something humans can actually read—in seconds, directly in your browser. We scan a website’s privacy policy, analyze it with AI, and present the results as clear summaries and intuitive icons—so you know exactly what’s happening with your data before you trust a site.

  • Instant Privacy Policy Analysis No more scrolling through 5,000 words of legal text.
    • Automatically detects and scans the website’s privacy policy
    • Uses AI to extract the important parts only
    • Works instantly while you browse—no copy/paste, no leaving the page
  • Simple AI-Powered Summaries We translate legal language into plain, human-friendly explanations.
    • Clear summaries of what data is collected
    • Easy-to-read explanations of how your data is used
    • Straightforward answers to who your data is shared with

    No legal background required. If a policy is vague, we tell you that too.

  • Visual Icons That Make Privacy Obvious Words are easy to hide behind. Icons aren’t.

    Privacy Lens uses clear visual indicators for you
