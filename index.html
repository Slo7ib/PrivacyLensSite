What is Privacy Lens?

Privacy Lens is a Chrome privacy extension that helps you understand what websites say they do with your data. It is designed as an AI browser privacy assistant: it highlights collection, purposes, and sharing in plain language—plus visual cues—so you can decide whether a site deserves your trust.

If you have searched for a privacy policy summary tool, a website privacy checker, or a terms and privacy summarizer workflow, Privacy Lens is built around the same job-to-be-done: reduce friction between you and the policy text publishers already published.

How it works

A simple flow engineered for scanning and comprehension—not for replacing your judgment.

  1. Step 1

    Detect the policy

FAQ

Answers mirror the structured FAQ data on this page for consistency with search engines.

What is Privacy Lens?

Privacy Lens is a free Chrome extension that acts as an AI browser privacy assistant. It finds a site’s privacy policy, analyzes it with AI, and presents a plain-language privacy policy summary plus structured signals about data collection, use, and sharing.

How does Privacy Lens summarize privacy policies?

When you use the extension on a supported page, it helps locate the policy text and runs an AI-powered analysis to extract the parts most people care about: categories of data, purposes, retention hints where available, and third parties. Results are shown as readable summaries and visual privacy indicators.

Is Privacy Lens only available as a Chrome privacy extension?

Today, Privacy Lens is distributed on the Chrome Web Store for Chromium-based Chrome browsers. Other browsers are not officially supported yet.

Is Privacy Lens a privacy policy analyzer or a website privacy checker?

It functions as both in practice: it is a privacy policy analyzer focused on policy text, and a website privacy checker in the sense that it helps you quickly understand a site’s stated practices before you proceed on that site.

Does Privacy Lens help with terms of service as well?

The product is optimized around privacy policies. Depending on the site and version, related legal pages may be less thoroughly covered than the primary privacy policy.

Is Privacy Lens free?

Privacy Lens is offered as a free extension. Optional donations may be available to support development, but core functionality is aimed at staying accessible.

Does Privacy Lens replace reading the full policy or legal advice?

No. Privacy Lens is an informational assistant designed to improve transparency and save time. It does not replace your own review, company disclosures, or professional legal advice where that is required.

How should I interpret AI-generated summaries?

AI outputs can be incomplete or imperfect—especially when a policy is vague, changes frequently, or uses non-standard language. Use summaries as a starting point and verify important details on critical flows like payments, health, or children’s data.

Where can I read Privacy Lens privacy practices and updates?

Use the Privacy Policy and What’s New pages linked in the site footer and navigation. Those pages describe site/extension policies and release notes context.

Where do I install Privacy Lens?

Install Privacy Lens from the official Chrome Web Store listing linked from this page. Prefer the official store URL to avoid impersonation.
